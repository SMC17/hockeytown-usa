The pipeline is a timing problem before it is a scouting problem.

[[player:gavin-mckenna]] sits on the [[draft:nhl-2026|2026 draft]] calendar and a [[commitment:mckenna-penn-state|Penn State commitment]]. That is two clocks. The NCAA season will tell a development story. The draft will assign NHL rights. Until then, Hockey Graph stores him as a Player with a `committed_to` edge into [[team:penn-state-nittany-lions]] — not as a rumor with a ranking bolted on.

[[player:cole-eiserman]] is the other shape. [[team:new-york-islanders]] already own the rights (`rights_owned_by`, [[draft:nhl-2024|2024]]). The body of work this winter is at [[team:boston-university-terriers]] via [[commitment:eiserman-bu]]. Publishing a "when does he sign" recap without those two objects is how a desk loses the year.

The calibration window is the overlap: college games that change an NHL org's patience, and NHL org patience that should not rewrite the college object. Hold this piece until the desk can attach real dates to both clocks. The graph already has the seats.
