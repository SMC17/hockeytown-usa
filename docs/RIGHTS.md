# Rights

Hockey Graph does not pirate highlights, rebroadcasts, or ripped game footage.

- `Video.rights` is `official` | `licensed` | `none`.
- A node with `rights: "none"` must never render a player.
- Phase 1 stores metadata and canonical official URLs only (team/NHL properties). Map and My Hockey stubs are geography/identity — they do not imply live NHL rights footage.
- Quotes are desk stubs or clearly sourced; they are not transcripts scraped from broadcasts.
- Seed contracts/AAVs are labeled `illustrative` until a licensed cap feed exists.

If a later front end needs clip playback, it goes through a licensed partner. The graph can hold the refusal.
